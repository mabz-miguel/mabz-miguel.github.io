# Career Audit 2026 — Shared Context

## Goal
Audit and reposition Miguel Ángel Ballesteros Zafra's professional presence to improve his chances of securing a new role.

The audit treats the following as one professional positioning system:
- Portfolio
- CV
- LinkedIn
- Target roles / market fit
- Portfolio UX/UI

## Core question
If a recruiter or hiring manager spends 30 seconds with Miguel Ángel's profile, what professional do they understand he is, what problems do they believe he solves, and for which roles would they consider interviewing him?

## Audit principles
1. Do not redesign before defining the positioning problem.
2. Separate evidence from assumptions.
3. Prioritize clarity, credibility, relevance and seniority over visual novelty.
4. Evaluate the portfolio as a recruitment product, not only as a design artifact.
5. Evaluate CV, LinkedIn and portfolio for consistency while allowing each channel to serve a different depth of information.
6. Validate recommendations later against real job descriptions.

## Independent-review rule
ChatGPT and Claude must perform their first audits independently.

- ChatGPT should not read Claude's conclusions before completing its initial audit.
- Claude should not read ChatGPT's conclusions before completing its initial audit.
- Cross-review happens only after both first-pass audits are complete.

This is intentional: disagreement is useful evidence and should not be suppressed.

## LinkedIn limitation for ChatGPT
The public LinkedIn URL can be partially indexed externally, but LinkedIn restricts full unauthenticated access. A complete audit should therefore use a LinkedIn PDF export and/or screenshots of the relevant sections.
