# ChatGPT Independent Audit — 02 Portfolio Content

Status: first-pass independent audit. Claude's audit has not been read.

## Executive diagnosis

The portfolio has credible raw material: strong brand exposure, a differentiated AI/production-systems angle and enough technical depth to feel substantive. The content problem is **conversion of experience into hiring evidence**.

At present, the site is better at saying “this person operates around sophisticated digital production and AI” than at proving “this is the exact level of ownership, impact and role you should hire him for.”

## Content hierarchy — current recruiting effect

### Hero
The hero identity “AI-Driven Digital Production Systems” is memorable but abstract. It does not complete the recruiter sentence:

> “Miguel is a ______ who helps ______ achieve ______.”

This is the most important content gap because every later section inherits that ambiguity.

### Selected Work
“Selected Work” is the right concept, but the projects must function as **evidence for the target role**, not merely as examples of interesting work.

A strong case study for this profile should make six things instantly scannable:
- problem,
- Miguel's role,
- ownership/decision,
- stakeholders/scale,
- solution/approach,
- result.

The current audit finds that the portfolio needs stronger emphasis on the middle three: **role, ownership and outcome**.

### Clients & collaborations
This section provides useful trust through recognizable brands, but the current long logo treatment risks becoming proof by association.

Brand names are powerful only when the visitor can connect at least the most important ones to concrete work and contribution.

### Contact
The line “Any industry, any scale — if the challenge is right, let's talk.” is polished but strategically broad. It does not reinforce the professional category or problem space established above.

## Priority issues

### CONT-P0-01 — Hero does not define the hireable role
**Severity:** P0  
**Confidence:** high

**Evidence:** “AI-Driven Digital Production Systems.”

**Hiring impact:** Strong concept, weak classification. A recruiter may admire the proposition and still not know which role it maps to.

**Direction:** Final hero should eventually contain both a recognizable professional category and a differentiated proposition. Do not write the final copy until market-fit work is complete.

---

### CONT-P0-02 — Case studies need explicit ownership
**Severity:** P0  
**Confidence:** medium-high

**Hiring impact:** Senior candidates are evaluated on what they owned, decided, coordinated or changed. If case studies focus primarily on the artifact/solution, the profile can be read as an expert executor even when the real work was broader.

**Required fields for every important project:**
- My role
- Context/problem
- What I owned
- Key decisions / trade-offs
- Stakeholders/team
- Outcome
- Scale/metric
- What this proves about me

---

### CONT-P0-03 — Outcomes must become first-class content
**Severity:** P0  
**Confidence:** medium-high

**Hiring impact:** Technical sophistication without measurable outcome is harder to value commercially.

**Direction:** Search existing evidence before rewriting. Useful outcome types include:
- time/effort reduction,
- quality/error reduction,
- adoption,
- number of people trained,
- number of assets/pages/campaigns/markets,
- standardized workflow,
- cost/estimation improvement,
- faster decision-making,
- stakeholder alignment,
- pilot-to-real-use progression.

Never fabricate metrics. A concrete qualitative outcome is better than an invented percentage.

---

### CONT-P1-04 — Brand logos need contextual proof
**Severity:** P1  
**Confidence:** high

**Evidence:** A large “Clients & collaborations” logo strip includes multiple global brands.

**Hiring impact:** Logos build initial credibility but create uncertainty about actual contribution when not tied to cases.

**Direction:** Keep a smaller set of high-value brands and make the strongest brand relationships traceable to specific work where confidentiality permits.

---

### CONT-P1-05 — The professional evolution story needs a clear causal arc
**Severity:** P1  
**Confidence:** medium

The About section contains an “I started…” narrative. That is potentially valuable because Miguel's background is genuinely evolutionary: deep production/3D knowledge → workflow understanding → automation/AI → broader project/product/people responsibility.

**Risk:** If told chronologically without a thesis, it can sound like a sequence of skills accumulated over time.

**Better narrative direction:**

> Deep production expertise gave me visibility into systemic problems. I progressively moved from executing production to designing better workflows, automating quality and scale, enabling teams, and taking broader ownership of how work gets delivered.

This is not proposed final copy; it is the strategic arc to prove.

---

### CONT-P1-06 — Current breadth needs editorial selection
**Severity:** P1  
**Confidence:** high

Not every technically impressive project should receive equal portfolio weight. The chosen projects should form an argument for the next role.

**Likely strong evidence categories to investigate:**
1. Intelligent Content QA System — automation, product/problem framing, real production validation.
2. Marketing Operations Estimator — business/process/estimation and cross-capability thinking.
3. AI enablement / workshops / training — adoption, facilitation, leadership.
4. A strong CGI/automotive production case — domain credibility and technical depth.
5. A GenAI/innovation case with external/business validation — innovation-to-value story.

Final selection must wait for evidence quality and target-role validation.

---

### CONT-P1-07 — Training/certification should support, not lead, the story
**Severity:** P1  
**Confidence:** medium

Project Management and Product Management training is useful, especially for a transition in perceived role. But certifications cannot substitute for proof of real ownership.

**Direction:** Present certifications as reinforcement after demonstrated project/product behaviors.

---

### CONT-P1-08 — CTA is too sector-agnostic and role-agnostic
**Severity:** P1  
**Confidence:** high

**Evidence:** “Any industry, any scale — if the challenge is right, let's talk.”

**Hiring impact:** It communicates confidence but not specialization.

**Direction:** Later make the CTA reinforce the problem class Miguel wants to be contacted about, while leaving industry open if desired.

---

### CONT-P2-09 — Contact exposes direct phone/email in page source
**Severity:** P2  
**Confidence:** high

This is not a recruiting blocker, but public source exposure increases spam/scraping risk. Decide later whether phone is necessary at portfolio stage.

## Case-study evaluation framework for the next pass

Every candidate project should be scored 0–5 on:

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Role relevance | Does it prove the target role? |
| Ownership | Is Miguel's personal responsibility unambiguous? |
| Business impact | Is there a real outcome? |
| Scale | Does it show meaningful complexity, audience, markets or volume? |
| Leadership | Does it show coordination, influence, facilitation or decisions? |
| Differentiation | Could many candidates tell the same story? |
| Evidence | Can claims be substantiated without confidentiality problems? |

Projects with the highest total should become the primary portfolio narrative. Others can be secondary work or removed.

## Content strengths to protect

- AI is connected to production problems rather than presented as generic tool enthusiasm.
- Global-brand exposure creates real credibility.
- The portfolio already attempts a systems-level narrative.
- There is a visible selected-work structure rather than a simple CV clone.
- The site has a direct LinkedIn/CV/contact route.

## Content conclusion

The next version should contain **less equal-weight breadth and more structured proof**.

The desired recruiting progression is:

> recognizable role → differentiated value → 3–4 proof cases → ownership/impact → domain breadth → contact.

The content audit should be considered incomplete until the embedded CV and full LinkedIn profile are reviewed, because those surfaces may either reinforce or contradict the portfolio narrative.
