## Recommended target hierarchy

### Primary target family — strongest evidence / market fit

1. **AI & Digital Innovation Manager**
2. **AI / Digital Transformation Project Manager**
3. **AI Adoption & Transformation Manager**
4. **Product Operations Manager** (especially content, digital operations, AI-assisted workflows)

These role families best combine Miguel's real evidence:
- operational/process transformation;
- GenAI adoption;
- discovery/workshops;
- MVP definition and prototyping;
- project coordination;
- stakeholder management;
- training/capability building;
- content QA / estimation / workflow systems;
- technical fluency without requiring pure engineering leadership.

### Secondary / stretch family

- AI Transformation Manager (enterprise strategy/governance)
- Technical Program Manager
- Transformation Program Manager
- Innovation Program Manager

These become more realistic as Miguel strengthens:
- budgeting/business cases/ROI;
- formal program governance;
- risk/dependency management;
- senior stakeholder/client exposure;
- change management at scale;
- English C1.

### Do not use as primary positioning today

- Generic Product Manager
- AI Strategy Manager
- IT Transformation Manager
- Head of AI / Head of Transformation
- Senior Technical Program Manager as a blanket label

The issue is not capability potential; it is evidence alignment. These titles trigger expectations that the current CV cannot consistently prove yet.

## Market gaps that are real, not copywriting problems

### English C1
Several relevant transformation roles explicitly require C1. This is a genuine development priority.

### Business case / financial ownership
High-level transformation roles increasingly ask for ROI, value realization, budgets and portfolio prioritization. Miguel's plan to practice estimation, budgets and business management directly addresses this gap.

### Enterprise governance
Senior AI transformation roles ask for operating models, AI governance, policy, risk and responsible-AI structures. Current evidence is stronger in adoption, workflows, MVPs and operational systems.

### Formal executive/client exposure
The founder period proves direct client ownership, but current-role evidence should show as much senior stakeholder exposure as is truthful. This is a market-relevant gap for consulting-manager roles.

### Formal project/program evidence
The CV should name concrete planning/governance activities where true: scope, milestones, dependencies, risks, status reporting, prioritization, acceptance criteria, stakeholder alignment and delivery decisions.

## Strategic conclusion

The market does not support positioning Miguel as simply a senior 3D/creative technologist, nor does it yet justify an unrestricted `AI Strategy / Transformation Leader` identity.

The strongest market story is:

> A manager-level digital transformation and AI-adoption professional who combines operational/process redesign, project delivery, stakeholder facilitation and hands-on technical understanding to turn fragmented production needs into scalable systems, MVPs and measurable improvements.

That story is differentiated, credible and maps to current vacancies. The final exact title should be chosen after cross-review with Claude and Codex, but the market evidence strongly favors transformation/innovation/project/product-operations over generic Product Manager or pure technical leadership.
