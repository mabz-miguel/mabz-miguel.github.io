# 05 — LinkedIn Audit

## Scope and limitation

This is an independent first-pass audit based on the publicly crawlable LinkedIn profile, which exposes the About opening, activity, employer, education/certifications and language level. LinkedIn blocks full unauthenticated retrieval of the detailed Experience section, so this document separates confirmed observations from recommendations that require a later profile export/manual review.

## Executive verdict

LinkedIn is already moving in the right direction: the public About begins with `Lidero iniciativas de transformación digital y de IA...`, which is materially clearer than a purely creative/3D identity. The remaining risk is narrative dilution: recent activity still mixes AI/3D/VFX exploration, workshops and broader transformation. That breadth is valuable, but without deliberate curation a recruiter may still classify Miguel as a senior creative technologist who is moving into management rather than as a transformation/innovation manager who happens to have deep production knowledge.

## P0 — The first-screen hiring category must match CV and portfolio

The final LinkedIn headline should use the same primary market category chosen for the CV and portfolio. It should not introduce a third label.

Recommended structure:

> [Primary role family] | GenAI Adoption | Operational Transformation | Cross-functional Delivery

Possible role-family candidates to validate against the market:

- AI & Digital Innovation Manager
- AI / Digital Transformation Project Manager
- AI Adoption & Transformation Manager
- Product Operations / Digital Operations Manager

Avoid stacking every identity (`AI`, `Product`, `Project`, `UX`, `3D`, `Innovation`, `Transformation`) into the headline.

## P0 — Featured content should control the narrative

The visible activity includes a post about AI redefining 3D/VFX pipelines and a Paid Media workshop. Both are useful, but the 3D/VFX post can pull the recruiter back toward an execution-specialist category if it is the most visible proof.

The Featured section should deliberately present 3–5 items that prove the target identity, ideally:

1. operational transformation / workshop case;
2. automated content QA system;
3. estimation / process system;
4. AI adoption / capability enablement;
5. one technical/creative AI case as evidence of depth, not as the main identity.

## P1 — About should tell a progression story, not a capability inventory

The public About opening is directionally strong. The full About should explain the career transition in a credible way:

- long foundation in digital production, design and emerging technology;
- growing ownership of teams, clients, workflows and delivery;
- current focus on AI adoption, transformation and operational systems;
- ability to bridge business, creative and technical teams;
- concrete proof through workshops, MVPs, QA, estimation and capability building.

This makes the 20+ years an asset rather than a source of ambiguity.

## P1 — Experience descriptions should use outcome-based bullets

The public crawler does not expose the detailed Accenture experience entries reliably. When the profile is reviewed manually/exported, ensure the current role does not read like a list of production tasks.

The current role should emphasize:

- initiatives led / coordinated;
- stakeholders and team scale;
- problems diagnosed;
- processes redesigned;
- MVPs or systems defined;
- adoption / training;
- measurable impact;
- ownership and decision-making.

Deep 3D/production execution should be compressed into evidence of domain understanding unless the target vacancy specifically values it.

## P1 — Founder history is a strategic asset and should be visible

BlackSheepStudio proves direct client relationship management, scope ownership, planning, delivery and coordination. This is highly relevant because current corporate work may offer limited direct client-facing exposure.

LinkedIn should make this period easy to understand as management/ownership experience, not simply freelance creative work.

## P1 — Skills should be curated to the target job family

Top visible skills should support the chosen category. Suggested top cluster:

- Digital Transformation
- Generative AI / AI Adoption
- Project Management
- Stakeholder Management
- Process Improvement
- Cross-functional Leadership
- Innovation Management
- Workshop Facilitation / Design Thinking
- Product Discovery / MVP Definition
- Operations / Workflow Optimization

Production tools and 3D skills can remain lower in the profile as differentiators.

## P1 — Activity strategy should reinforce the job search

Future posts should increasingly demonstrate manager-level thinking:

- how an operational problem was framed;
- how stakeholders were aligned;
- how a workflow was redesigned;
- what was learned from an MVP;
- how AI adoption was measured;
- trade-offs, governance and change management;
- lessons from leading workshops or teams.

Technical exploration remains useful, but should answer `why this matters for teams/business` rather than only `what the technology can do`.

## P2 — Language consistency

The public profile content is primarily Spanish while the portfolio and CV are English. This is not inherently wrong for a Madrid-based search, but the three surfaces should feel intentional.

If targeting international roles in Spain/Europe, an English headline/About is likely the cleaner default. A Spanish profile can still work if the target market is mainly Spain, but role terminology should remain consistent across all three assets.

## P2 — Certifications support the transition well

Public LinkedIn data confirms Product Management training at ESIC, Google Project Management certifications and Design Thinking. These are useful credibility bridges into the target role family, but should not be used to imply formal Product Manager experience that has not occurred.

## P2 — English level

LinkedIn publicly reports English as `Competencia profesional completa`. The CV states B2 with daily international use. Ensure these two descriptions are mutually defensible; LinkedIn proficiency labels are broad, but the overall application package should not create the impression of C1 if that is not yet the case.

## Full-audit items still requiring direct LinkedIn export/manual access

- exact current headline;
- full About text;
- exact wording/order of Experience entries;
- top Skills and endorsements;
- Featured section;
- Recommendations;
- profile photo/banner visual positioning;
- Open to Work / job preferences;
- exact language/version settings.

## Overall assessment

**Current visible strategic direction: promising, but not yet fully verifiable.**

The strongest opportunity is to use LinkedIn as the most recruiter-native version of the story: one clear target role, evidence of leadership/transformation, and curated proof. The profile should make the creative/technical background feel like an unusual advantage inside a management/transformation narrative, not a competing identity.
