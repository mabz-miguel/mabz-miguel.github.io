# Final Positioning Decision — Digital Project Manager

**Status:** APPROVED by Miguel Ángel Ballesteros and ready for implementation.

## Primary market identity

**Digital Project Manager**

Recommended support line:

> **Digital Production · AI-Enabled Workflows · Cross-functional Delivery**

## Why this is the primary identity

This decision is based on recruiter demand and current-market role reality, not on creating a title that attempts to summarize every capability Miguel has.

`Digital Project Manager` is an established and recognizable market family. A recruiter understands the baseline responsibility immediately: translating needs into executable digital projects, coordinating stakeholders and multidisciplinary contributors, managing delivery and quality, and moving work from definition through implementation.

Miguel can defend this identity from work he has genuinely done:

- End-to-end digital project ownership through BlackSheepStudio: client requirements, planning, execution, scope, quality, timelines, collaborators and delivery.
- Cross-functional digital delivery across UX/UI, XR, 3D/CGI, realtime and digital-production environments.
- Current coordination of multidisciplinary teams and collaboration across technical, creative and business profiles.
- Translation of operational needs into practical solutions, MVPs and internal tools.
- Content QA, estimation-system and workflow initiatives.
- Stakeholder discovery and workshop facilitation.
- GenAI enablement, automation and AI-assisted workflows.
- Google Project Management certification and Product Management training.

The title does **not** depend on a company currently having a transformation programme, a broken process or a special AI-adoption initiative. Digital projects are a recurring business need. Transformation, process improvement, AI and automation can therefore appear as capabilities and differentiators rather than pretending to be Miguel's profession.

## Differentiation

Miguel should not be presented as a generic PM who arrived from project-management theory. His strongest differentiator is that he has **20+ years of hands-on digital-production experience** behind the management layer.

The narrative is:

> **Digital Project Manager with 20+ years of hands-on experience across digital production, 3D/CGI, XR and creative technology, now working across AI-enabled workflows, automation and operational improvement.**

This makes technical and production depth evidence of better project judgment rather than a separate professional identity.

## Adjacent roles

These remain valid vacancy families when the job description matches the evidence, but they are **not** the evergreen identity:

- Digital Content Project Manager
- Creative / Digital Production Manager
- Creative Operations Manager
- Digital Transformation Manager (vacancy-specific)
- GenAI Enablement / AI Adoption roles (vacancy-specific)

`Program Manager` is a realistic growth direction but is **not** the current primary claim. Enterprise-level programme governance, multi-workstream ownership, budget accountability and steering-level reporting need stronger evidence before making that the default identity.

## Titles not to use as the primary identity

- AI & Digital Transformation Lead / Manager
- AI Systems Lead
- Digital Operations & Innovation Manager
- Operations Process Improvement Manager
- Creative Technologist / Creative Technology Lead
- Head / Director titles without matching scope evidence

## Implementation rule

Portfolio, CV and LinkedIn must all answer the same five-second recruiter question:

> **Miguel is a Digital Project Manager. He knows how to take digital work from need to delivery, coordinate different profiles, understand the production reality underneath it, and use AI / automation when those capabilities genuinely improve the project.**

AI, transformation, operations, process improvement, 3D/CGI and XR are proof and specialization layers. They must not obscure the primary role.
