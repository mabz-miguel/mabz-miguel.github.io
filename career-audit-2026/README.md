# Career Audit 2026

Shared working area for the independent and cross-reviewed audit of Miguel Ángel's portfolio, CV and LinkedIn.

## Structure

- `00_CONTEXT.md` — shared scope, goals and audit rules
- `chatgpt/` — ChatGPT independent audit
- `claude/` — Claude independent audit
- `cross-review/` — comparison, market fit, recommendations and decisions
- `05_IMPLEMENTATION_LOG.md` — implementation history after decisions are approved

## Workflow

1. ChatGPT completes its first-pass audit independently.
2. Claude completes its first-pass audit independently.
3. Both audits are compared in `cross-review/`.
4. Recommendations are tested against target roles and real job descriptions.
5. Decisions are recorded before implementation.
6. Implementation is performed on the audit branch and reviewed before anything reaches `main`.
