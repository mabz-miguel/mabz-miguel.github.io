# Positioning challenge for Claude — market-relevant, grounded, not hype

Date: 2026-09-04

## Context
Miguel has raised a valid concern with the current cross-review recommendation `AI Adoption & Digital Transformation Manager`:

> It risks becoming too generic and AI-heavy. AI is still an abstract label, and the positioning may erase the strongest hard-earned differentiator in his background: 20+ years across 3D/CGI, realtime, XR, digital production, creative technology and production workflows.

The goal is **not** to return to a 3D Artist / CGI Specialist identity. The goal is to find a role family that:

1. exists in the market now;
2. is recruiter-searchable;
3. uses his real current strengths;
4. makes his 3D / digital-production / creative-tech background an advantage rather than baggage;
5. does not overclaim AI strategy, engineering, product leadership or enterprise transformation ownership he cannot yet prove;
6. gives him realistic room to move upward into management, innovation and transformation.

## The key question

What should Miguel's **primary external professional identity** be if the objective is:

- to target roles that genuinely exist now;

- to use 3D/CGI/realtime/digital-production depth as a differentiator;

## Candidate directions to challenge

Do not accept these automatically. Rank and critique them:

1. **Digital Transformation & AI Adoption Manager**
   - pro: searchable, broad market;
   - con: may be too generic and hide domain depth.

2. **Creative Technology & Digital Transformation Manager**
   - pro: reflects real background and management direction;
   - con: may be less common as an exact title.

3. **Digital Production & Creative Technology Manager | AI Adoption & Transformation**
   - pro: highly grounded in evidence;
   - con: may keep him too close to production rather than transformation.

4. **MarTech & Creative Technology Manager / Lead**
   - pro: a real current job family and strong overlap with marketing-operations context;

6. **AI Adoption & Digital Production Transformation Manager**
   - pro: connects current demand with domain expertise;
