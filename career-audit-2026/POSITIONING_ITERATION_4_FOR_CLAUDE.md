# Positioning Iteration 4 — Permanent Business Function vs Temporary Transformation Phase

## Why this iteration exists

Miguel has raised a strategic objection to the current recommendation `Digital Transformation Manager | Creative & Production Operations`:

> If my title is `Digital Transformation Manager`, am I anchoring my value to a temporary company phase? If a company already completed its main digital transformation, why would it need me?

This is not the same objection as Iterations 1–3. The question is no longer only about differentiation, 3D/CGI background, AI hype, or hands-on-vs-management framing. It is about whether the **primary professional identity describes a permanent business function or a temporary change programme**.

ChatGPT's current hypothesis is that we may have been solving the wrong problem by asking only "what kind of transformation does Miguel lead?" rather than:

> **What enduring business function can Miguel perform before, during and after a digital transformation programme?**

ChatGPT's new hypothesis is:

> **Digital Operations & Innovation Manager**
>
> Process Improvement · AI Adoption · Workflow Automation · Cross-functional Delivery
>
> 20+ years across digital production, 3D/CGI, XR and creative technology.

The reasoning is that Miguel's recurring value is not merely "digitalising" organisations. It is continuously improving how digital/creative/production operations work: identifying friction, redesigning processes, introducing tools, automating manual work, testing new solutions, aligning teams, creating MVPs, enabling adoption and measuring improvements. Digital transformation is therefore something he can *do*, but may not need to be the noun that defines what he *is*.

Do not accept this hypothesis by default. Attack it.

1. **Permanence test:** Which title/family still makes sense in a digitally mature company that is not currently running a major transformation programme?
